VYNT Billing

Overview

VYNT uses subscription-based access for premium intelligence and automation features.

Trial

The planned VYNT trial includes:

* 7 days
* 30 AI credits

Trial expiration is verified server-side.

Historical analytics remain available after trial expiration where applicable.

VYNT Pro

VYNT Pro is planned as a subscription product priced at:

$50/month

The exact credit allowance and feature configuration are controlled by the platform.

Credits

AI-heavy actions can consume credits.

Example actions include:

Action	Credits
AI Content Analysis	1
Keyword Research	1
Caption Optimization	1
Content Ideas	1
Forecast	1
Full Strategy	3
Deep Analysis	3

Credit costs may change as the product evolves.

Subscription States

VYNT can support:

* Trial
* Active
* Past Due
* Cancelled
* Expired

Payment Provider

VYNT is designed with a billing-provider abstraction.

Stripe may be used for production billing where configured.

Demo Mode

Development environments may use a clearly labeled demo billing mode when production billing credentials are unavailable.

Demo billing should never be represented as a real production payment.

Paywall

Premium features should be protected individually rather than unnecessarily blocking access to historical account data.

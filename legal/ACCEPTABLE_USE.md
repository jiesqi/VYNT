VYNT Acceptable Use Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026
Last Updated: September 20, 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy (“AUP”) describes prohibited uses of VYNT Services.

1. Lawful Use

Users must use VYNT in compliance with applicable laws, regulations, contracts, and third-party platform policies.

2. Prohibited Activities

Users must not use VYNT to:

* Commit or facilitate unlawful activity
* Access systems without authorization
* Circumvent authentication
* Circumvent subscription or licensing controls
* Distribute malware
* Conduct unauthorized credential attacks
* Abuse APIs
* Perform denial-of-service attacks
* Attempt to compromise VYNT infrastructure
* Interfere with other users
* Harvest information without authorization
* Upload malicious code
* Impersonate another person or organization
* Infringe intellectual-property rights
* Violate privacy rights
* Distribute unlawful content
* Circumvent third-party platform restrictions

3. Platform Compliance

Users are responsible for complying with the terms and policies of platforms connected to VYNT.

VYNT does not authorize users to bypass platform restrictions.

4. Automation

Automation must be configured and used responsibly.

Users must not configure VYNT automation to:

* Spam third parties
* Flood external services
* Circumvent rate limits
* Publish unauthorized material
* Perform actions the user is not authorized to perform

5. Content

Users are responsible for content they submit, process, publish, or distribute through VYNT.

Users must have appropriate rights and permissions for content they process.

6. Security

Users must not attempt to:

* Obtain another user’s credentials
* Extract secrets
* Circumvent license verification
* Disable security controls
* Exploit vulnerabilities for unauthorized purposes
* Reverse engineer security mechanisms where prohibited by law

Good-faith security research should be reported through the appropriate security reporting channel.

7. Enforcement

VYNT may investigate suspected violations and may take appropriate action, including:

* Warning
* Rate limiting
* Temporary suspension
* Feature restriction
* License suspension
* Account termination

Actions will be considered based on the circumstances and applicable law.

8. Changes

VYNT may update this policy as the Services and applicable requirements evolve.

VYNT Third-Party Services

Effective Date: September 20, 2026
Last Updated: September 20, 2026

VYNT may integrate with third-party platforms and services to provide authentication, payment processing, AI capabilities, infrastructure, analytics, communication, content distribution, and other functionality.

1. Independent Services

Third-party services are independently operated and may have their own:

* Terms of service
* Privacy policies
* API requirements
* Security practices
* Rate limits
* Data-retention practices
* Availability requirements

VYNT does not control independent third-party services.

2. Platform Integrations

VYNT may integrate with services such as:

* TikTok
* YouTube
* Instagram
* Payment providers
* AI providers
* Cloud infrastructure providers
* Analytics providers
* Email providers
* RSS consumers
* Webhook destinations
* Custom APIs

The specific services used by VYNT may change.

3. Authorization

Users are responsible for ensuring that they have permission to connect third-party accounts to VYNT.

Users should review the permissions requested during authorization.

4. Third-Party Terms

Using VYNT with a third-party service does not replace the third party’s own terms or policies.

If a third-party platform restricts an action, VYNT does not grant permission to bypass that restriction.

5. Third-Party Availability

VYNT cannot guarantee continuous availability of third-party services.

Changes to third-party APIs, policies, authentication systems, pricing, or infrastructure may affect VYNT functionality.

6. Third-Party Content

Content obtained from third-party services may remain subject to the rights and licenses of the relevant third party or content owner.

VYNT does not claim ownership of third-party content merely because it is processed through VYNT.

7. Third-Party AI Services

Where external AI providers are used, submitted information may be processed according to the applicable provider configuration and agreement.

VYNT should configure AI integrations in accordance with its privacy and security requirements.

8. Payment Providers

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers.

VYNT may receive payment-related status and transaction metadata without storing complete payment-card information.

9. Changes

The third-party services used by VYNT may change as the product evolves.
